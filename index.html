About SoundLens

Understand your sound before you release it.

SoundLens analyzes your beat or song and gives artist match, mix issues, arrangement feedback, and clear next steps.

@soundlensapp_

Who needs this?

Artists

Check whether your track is loud enough, clear enough, and close to the sound you are aiming for.

Producers

Get fast feedback on low end, bounce, brightness, arrangement, and artist-style direction.

Engineers

Use SoundLens as a second opinion before sending a rough mix, master, or revision.

How does it work?

1. Upload audio

Choose a WAV, MP3, M4A, AAC, FLAC, or OGG file.

2. SoundLens analyzes

It reads loudness, frequency balance, rhythm, arrangement, and sonic fingerprints.

3. Get fixes

See the biggest problems first, then artist match and advanced EQ guidance.
